How to & Tips

Make a paper specimen jar with a glass jar, lightweight paper, scissors and tape.

  1. Print at actual size

    Choose the pages for your paper size, A4 or US Letter. Select Actual size or 100% in the print settings. Lightweight paper curves easily inside the jar.

  2. Try the wrap inside the jar

    Cut out the illustrated wrap and gently curve it with the artwork facing outward. Slip it into a clean, dry jar. Check the height and overlap before adding tape.

  3. Hide the seam

    Turn the wrap until your favourite detail faces the front. Tape the ends together at the back. For a wider jar, use the extension panel to bridge the gap.

  4. Place the label

    Stand the jar on a level surface. Rest the label against the glass to check its position, then attach it. Finish with the small brand sticker on the lid or side.
