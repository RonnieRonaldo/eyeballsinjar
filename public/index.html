Make your eyeball jar

Curve the paper so the eyes face outward. Turn the jar until your favourite cluster looks straight back at you, then add the outer label.

  1. Print the insert and labels on lightweight paper.
  2. Trim the wrap, test the fit and tape the ends inside the jar.
  3. Attach the outer label and place the jar in your cabinet.
